Product Support Policy

Refunds are available within 30 days after purchase.
To request a refund, the customer must contact support@example.com.
Refunds are processed within 5 business days.

The Pro plan costs $20 per month.
The Team plan costs $50 per month and supports up to 10 users.

Priority support is available for Team plan customers from Monday to Friday.
